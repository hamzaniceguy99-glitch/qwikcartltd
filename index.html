Who are you, legally?

Qwik Cart Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 15832592, registered office 75 Holkham Avenue, Leicester, LE4 9US. All of it is verifiable on the Companies House register.

Can I cancel an order?

If you're buying as a consumer, yes — you have 14 days from delivery to cancel most orders under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, and we don't make that difficult.

What if something arrives faulty?

You have separate rights for faulty or misdescribed goods, independent of the 14-day cancellation right. Email us and we'll sort it rather than quoting policy at you.
